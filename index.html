Lake Cisadane Water Turbine

A simple turbine that uses moving water to make electricity for villagers.

Our idea

Some villages near the lake still have little or unstable electricity. But the water around them is always moving. Our idea is to place a water turbine in the flowing water and use that energy to make electricity.

How it works

1. Water flows

Moving water pushes against the blades of the turbine.

2. Turbine spins

The blades turn a shaft connected to a small generator.

3. Electricity is made

The generator produces electricity that can be used for lights and charging phones.

Why it is useful

  • It uses water energy, which is renewable and keeps flowing.
  • It makes no smoke, so it is clean for the environment.
  • It can help villagers have light at night for studying.
  • It uses a resource that is already there, so it can be low cost.
